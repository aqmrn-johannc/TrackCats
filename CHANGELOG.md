# Update log

The same notes appear inside the app under More, then "What's new".

## 1.0.3 (2026-11-27)

- **Banner calendar**: on the Banners tab, switch to Calendar to see open and upcoming banners week by week, with their set name, dates, guaranteed draws, your wishlist cats and how many ubers are new for you.
- **Wishlist routes**: the Plan tab now lists every wishlist cat with the cheapest way to reach it and whether you can afford it.
- **Cat details**: tap any cat to see its forms, where to get it, your cheapest route, and to mark it owned or wishlisted. A button looks it up on the wiki.
- **Mark many cats at once**: on My cats, narrow the list with search, rarity or a set, then mark or unmark everything shown.
- **Set progress**: My cats has a Sets view showing how many ubers of each set you own and which are missing. Tap a set to list its cats.
- **Light or dark**: choose it under More, then Display, or keep following the phone.
- When confirming rolls you can now choose whether the cats are marked as owned (they still are by default).

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-27)

- **Rolls tab**: the guaranteed-draw card now sits right under the banner, above your next rolls. Banners without one say "This banner doesn't have a guaranteed uber."
- **Track table**: it now lives in a pull-up tab at the bottom of the Rolls tab instead of a card at the end of the list. Swipe it up (or tap it) to see the full table for your next 100 positions; swipe down to put it away.
- **Theme colour**: pick any colour for the app with a colour wheel under More, then Display. Buttons, titles and the background tint follow it, and it is adjusted slightly when needed so text stays readable.
- **Header removed**: the TrackCats bar at the top of every screen is gone, leaving more room for content.
- The "Advanced view" switch is now "Track positions on rolls"; the track table no longer depends on it.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-27)

- **Cat pictures**: every cat now shows its icon in roll lists, plans, pickers, history and My cats.
- **Banner art**: banners show their artwork on the Rolls tab, the Banners tab and when choosing a banner.
- **New look**: a soft gradient background, every section in its own card, and a warmer orange accent in place of the flat yellow.
- **Game-style lettering** for titles, buttons and big results; normal lettering stays for explanations so they remain easy to read.
- **Smooth motion**: pages and tabs slide and fade, lists glide when they change, and cards open and close with animation.
- **Faster**: the app is now built in optimised mode, so scrolling and navigation are noticeably smoother. The download shrank from 24 MB to 15 MB.
- **Tabs remember where you were**: switching tabs and coming back keeps your scroll position.
- Pictures download once (about 3 MB) and then work offline. Without internet you see names and rarity colours, as before.
- A "What's new" page, shown once after each update and always available under More.

Notes:

- No artwork is stored in this repository or in the APK. The app downloads cat icons and banner art from the community-run Battle Cats Wiki and keeps them on the phone.
- The title lettering is Lilita One, an open-licence font chosen for its resemblance to the game's style. The game's own font is not used.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-26)

- First version: guided seed search, next-rolls list, banner comparison, planner, "I rolled" sync with history and undo, My cats and wishlist, alerts, advanced track table, backup.
